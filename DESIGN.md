## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a clinical yet warm digital environment tailored for veterinary medical teams, pet parents, and clinic managers. Balancing high-trust medical utility with approachable empathy, the visual identity rejects cold, clinical sterility in favor of calm, reassuring professionalism.

The core design movement combines **Modern Healthcare SaaS** with **Soft Tactility**:
- **Clarity & Composure:** High contrast text hierarchy, airy negative space, and organized multi-step layouts that minimize cognitive load during stressful veterinary consultations or pet medical emergencies.
- **Friendly Precision:** Rounded physical surfaces, pill-shaped markers, soft contextual borders, and deliberate teal-and-navy accents that bridge veterinary clinical authority with affection for animal companions.
- **Functional Dependability:** Clean data tables, intuitive status badges, clear form elements, and high-legibility typographic hierarchies built to support rapid desktop input and accessible mobile companion views.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from clinical trust cues and the brand emblem's signature teal and deep maritime blue.

- **Primary Teal (`#14B8A6`) & Deep Teal (`#0D9488`):** The primary interaction anchor representing health, vitality, and gentle care. Used for primary CTAs, active selection indicators, and brand focal points.
- **Secondary Deep Navy (`#123B5D`):** The grounding architectural anchor representing veterinary authority, data integrity, and steadfast reliability. Applied to persistent navigation, key headers, and core structure.
- **Soft Teal Tints (`#F0FDFA`, `#CCFBF1`):** Subdued surface backgrounds, active table row highlights, and contextual callout panels that prevent visual fatigue.
- **Neutral Stack:**
  - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for soft contrast against cards.
  - Surface cards: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
  - Subtle borders: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for refined containment.
  - Primary text: `#0F2840` for maximum legibility without harsh black tone.
  - Secondary text: `#475569` (Slate 600) for metadata, timestamps, and field labels.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Confirmed & Available: `#10B981` (Emerald) with `#ECFDF5` background.
  - In Progress & Pending: `#F59E0B` (Amber) with `#FEF3C7` background.
  - Completed & Inactive: `#64748B` (Slate) with `#F1F5F9` background.
  - Cancelled & Critical: `#EF4444` (Rose/Red) with `#FEF2F2` background.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is the single typographic voice across headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its geometric clarity and softly sculpted counters echo the geometry of the brand's paw and location pin mark while preserving crisp legibility in tabular records and prescription details.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set with tighter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and assertive weights (SemiBold to ExtraBold) using deep navy `#0F2840` to project confidence and medical stability.
- **Body Text:** Maintains generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) for rapid scanning of pet histories, clinical notes, and onboarding guides.
- **Labels & Badges:** Use uppercase or title case with `letterSpacing: 0.04em` in compact sizes (`11px` to `13px`) for patient tags (e.g., Canine, Feline, Vaccinated).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt geometric spacing scale**, designed for a fluid, desktop-optimized SaaS workflow with responsive adaptations for clinic tablets and companion mobile screens.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and a max canvas width of `1440px`. Accommodates persistent left navigation bars (260px fixed width), calendar grids, and side-by-side patient dossiers.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with collapsing icon-rail navigation and `1.25rem` gutters, prioritizing one-handed touch input for veterinary technicians.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stream view with full-bleed cards, bottom navigation, `1rem` margins, and sticky bottom primary actions.
- **Multi-Step Flow Spacing:** Multi-step appointment and patient onboarding flows use a centered container maxing out at `680px`, structured with `space-xl` between sections and `space-md` between clustered form fields.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs **subtle ambient shadows** tinted with navy tones, paired with **crisp low-contrast outlines**. This ensures medical records and interactive states stay sharp without feeling detached from the canvas.

- **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background canvas `#F8FAFC` without shadow.
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** Main cards, data panels, and tables. Styled with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border and a soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04)`.
- **Raised / Hover Surface (Level 2):** Selectable appointment cards, hovered table rows, and interactive chips. Border turns to `#CBD5E1` with shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.05)`.
- **Floating / Overlay Surface (Level 3):** Dropdown menus, modal dialogs, drawer panels, and toast alerts. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.06)`. Modals feature a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`) with a navy wash: `rgba(15, 40, 64, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses the organic, friendly geometry of the paw icon while maintaining SaaS functional efficiency.

- **Primary Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** `rounded-md` (`0.5rem` / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (`0.75rem` / 12px) for structured, space-efficient data entry.
- **Container Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / 16px) to deliver an approachable, human, non-intimidating health care experience.
- **Badges, Status Indicators, & Chips:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to soften technical medical information and status labels.
- **Pet Avatars & Icon Containers:** Enclosed in continuous circular frames (`rounded-full`) or squircle containers with `rounded-xl`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#14B8A6`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight SemiBold, `rounded-xl`. Hover state deepens to `#0D9488` with a subtle elevation shift. Focus ring: `3px solid #CCFBF1`.
- **Secondary:** Background `#F0FDFA`, text `#0D9488`, border `1px solid #CCFBF1`. Hover transitions to `#CCFBF1`.
- **Outline / Neutral:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#123B5D`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover uses `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#EF4444`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover uses `#DC2626`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text & Select Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-xl`, padding `0 1rem`. Text `#0F2840`.
- **Placeholder:** Text `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border `#14B8A6`, paired with outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- **Error State:** Border `#EF4444`, outer glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`. Accompanying helper text in `#EF4444`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, label size `label-sm`.
- **Confirmed / Active:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, left-aligned 6px emerald dot.
- **Pending:** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, left-aligned 6px amber dot.
- **Completed:** Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, left-aligned 6px slate dot.
- **Cancelled:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, left-aligned 6px red dot.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** `rounded-md` (6px), size 20px x 20px, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. When checked, background fills `#14B8A6` with a white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Button:** Circular (`rounded-full`), size 20px x 20px, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state displays a `#14B8A6` perimeter with a concentric 8px `#14B8A6` center pill.

### Cards & Data Panels
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `rounded-2xl`, internal padding `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Pet Profile Card:** Features avatar anchor, species pill tag, key vitals row, and upcoming booking indicator separated by `#F1F5F9` hair dividers.

### Multi-Step Stepper Component
- Horizontal segmented flow for appointment booking and pet intake:
  - Active Step: Teal circle with white numeral, accompanied by a bold teal step title.
  - Completed Step: Navy or teal background with a check icon, connected via a solid `#14B8A6` line.
  - Upcoming Step: Slate ring `#CBD5E1` with muted gray label connected via a dashed `#E2E8F0` track.
